Incident case study / Supply-chain breach / November 2026

46,000 bookings.
Zero SIA systems breached.

For more than three years, attackers sat inside Amadeus, the booking platform behind many of the world's airlines. They walked away with Singapore Airlines passenger data without ever touching SIA's own network. This is how it happened, stage by stage.

Based on public reporting up to 18 November 2026. The investigation is ongoing. Every claim is tagged Confirmed or Inferred and linked to a source.
